# Privacy Policy for Syntax Downloader

**Effective Date:** November 13, 2026  
**Repository:** https://github.com/Harry2210/Syntax-Downloader  

Syntax Downloader ("we", "our", or "the extension") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data for the Syntax Downloader desktop application and browser extension.

## 1. Single Purpose
Syntax Downloader's sole purpose is to intercept, accelerate, and manage user-initiated file and media downloads by transferring download requests directly to your local Syntax Downloader desktop application.

## 2. No Data Collection or External Transmission
- We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).
- We do **not** sell, rent, monetize, or share your data with any third parties or advertisers.
- All download capture operations, session cookies, and referrer headers are processed **strictly locally** on your own computer between the browser extension and the local desktop application (`http://127.0.0.1:36855`). No telemetry, analytics, or user browsing logs are uploaded to any external servers.

## 3. Permissions Usage
- **downloads**: To detect and intercept browser downloads and transfer them to the desktop client.
- **contextMenus**: To provide "Download with Syntax" when right-clicking links and media.
- **notifications**: To notify you locally when a download link is captured.
- **storage**: To save your preferences (e.g. enabled/disabled toggles) locally in your browser.
- **activeTab & tabs**: To obtain the originating URL and filename for user-initiated downloads.
- **cookies**: To forward session cookies exclusively to the local download manager for authenticated downloads.
- **nativeMessaging & local host permissions**: To communicate directly with the local Syntax Downloader application on your machine.

## 4. Updates
Our application checks for version updates via GitHub Gist. No personal user data or machine identifiers are included in update checks.
